# CHIAREL Instagram

Adapts a generic Instagram-agent idea to this brand. It is a drafting and review skill, not an automation. Posting stays with the owner.

Builds on `small-business:social-content-engine` (cadence, formats) and `design` / `banner-design` (visual production). This skill adds what those do not know: the brand rules, the claims limits and the CHIAREL assets. Run `claim-audit` on any batch of posts before handing it over.

## 0. Before drafting, confirm three things (ask the owner if unknown)

1. **Launch state.** Stripe was in TEST MODE as of 2026-09-16. Do not write "shop now" or "available now" until the owner confirms live sales. Until then the call to action is the waitlist or the ritual page, never a checkout.
2. **Which products are public.** Essence, Terra Radiance Crème, N1 Neck & Décolleté Renewal Emulsion and the Recovery Masque are on the site. The Eye product is **CHIAREL Eye Treatment™** only after the owner clears the trademark and sets a launch date; until then do not announce it.
3. **Founding Members.** The current offer is numbered places with early access, not a discount. Check it is live and counsel has approved before promoting it. Never write "half price", "% off", or any pay-for-content or refund-for-video offer.

## 1. Voice

- Outcome language only: "supports", "optimizes", "helps the look of". Never "fixes", "miracle", "erases", "reverses".
- Banned words: Anti-Aging, Youth, Miracle, Lift, Wrinkle, Plump, Glow, Balm, Milk, Vitale, Chiarelle, Isola Vitale.
- Never state an age range to customers ("for 30-50+"). Translate to skin condition and texture: "skin that has lost some of its resilience".
- No "clean", "pure", "natural", "harsh chemicals", "chemical-free", or "unlike other brands". The formulas contain gums, emulsifiers, a preservative and Parfum, so these cannot be backed.
- The accord is described only as "the CHIAREL fragrance accord" until the supplier declaration lands.
- Calm, exact, unhurried. Short sentences. One idea per post. No exclamation marks, no emoji chains (one restrained mark is fine, none is better), no hype verbs.
- Names: CHIAREL™, The House of Clarity™, Advancing Cellular Clarity™. Product names are functional ("Neck & Décolleté Renewal Emulsion"), with no ingredient names in titles.
- Place: the label and wordmark line reads **ITALIA**. The story is **Isola del Liri**, made fresh to order. "An hour and a half from Rome" is fine as geography.
- Retired and rejected, never reuse: LIRI ROMA™, DWAT, "Cascata Red".

## 2. Claims

- **Owner-approved, use as written, never flag:** "Customer Tested" (backed by the customer-testing photos, kept at illustrative level) and "clinically studied ingredients".
- **Everything else is a fresh decision.** No "clinically proven", "dermatologist approved", "results in 48 hours", before/after claims beyond the approved customer-testing level, or numbers of any kind (no percentages, no ingredient percentages, no "n=").
- **Ingredients:** name key ingredients in plain words only. Do not publish full INCI lists (hidden until the supplier declarations are final). Say "L-Ornithine".
- **Free-from line (approved wording only):** no parabens, sulfates, mineral oil, silicones, denatured alcohol. Nothing broader.
- **Testimonials and UGC:** never invent them. Real customer content needs written permission, and any gift or incentive must be disclosed (#ad / paid partnership label). See the claims hierarchy at `07 Marketing/CHIAREL_Claims_Hierarchy_V1.0.md` (draft, tiered) before any efficacy line.
- **Competitors:** never name or compare.

## 3. Visuals

- **Doctrine:** 70% luxury editorial, 20% scientific precision, 10% human lifestyle.
- **Palette (Decision 002):** Cloud Dancer #F0F2EB, Red Ochre #9B4722 (site interface accent), Formal Garden #1F5129, Peach Dust #FAD6C9, Champagne Gold #D6C5A0 (typography and hardware accents; the gold used in the home intro drop is a product-matter gold). One colour, one job. No new reds or gradients.
- **Type:** Libre Bodoni for display, Jost for small text. Always name them by name in briefs.
- **Formats:** feed 4:5 (1080×1350), Reels and Stories 9:16 (1080×1920), carousel cover and slides 4:5. Keep key content inside the central safe area; Reels crop top and bottom.
- **Image rules:** packshots are transparent 1400×1400 PNGs; never bake a background into one. Every pack and label shows CHIAREL / **ITALIA**. Do not use any image that still reads ROME (Pomelli renders, the Copilot group shot, Amalfi Essence, old N1 jar scenes, `Hands holding_Neck & Decollete.png`) until it is relettered.
- **AI-generated imagery:** label photorealistic AI images with Instagram's AI label. Never present an AI-generated image as a customer or a real result.
- **Usable assets:** `public/assets/home/hero-hands-n1-wide.jpg` (hands and N1), `public/assets/products/n1-neck-decollete-marble-roses.jpg` (N1 with the Isola del Liri falls), `n1-neck-decollete-honeycomb.jpg`, `n1-neck-decollete-ambient.mp4`, the packshots, `chiarel-essence-studio.jpg`, `terra-radiance-creme-editorial.jpg` and `-capri.jpg`, `recovery-masque-studio.jpg`. Check the file reads ITALIA before use.

## 4. Content pillars (rotate, no more than two of the same in a row)

1. **The ritual** (AM/PM, how to apply, the order). Calm demonstration, hands and texture.
2. **Provenance:** Isola del Liri, the falls and the Liri water, made fresh to order. Place first, product second.
3. **The product up close:** one product, one detail (jar, bottle, texture, pump), no claim stacking.
4. **The science, plainly:** one named ingredient and what it is formulated to support. Mechanism language only, never a CHIAREL result.
5. **The house:** the formulator, the lab, the craft. Real people only with their consent.

Cadence for a pre-launch brand: 3 feed posts and 3 to 5 Stories a week, 1 Reel a week. Quality over volume.

## 5. Output format (always a draft pack, never a post)

For each item provide:

- **Format and slot** (feed / carousel / Reel / Story, day).
- **Visual** (which asset, crop, or a brief for a new one).
- **Caption** in two parts: the first line carries the idea and must hold on its own (it is all that shows before "more"); then 2 to 4 short lines. End with a quiet call to action matched to launch state (section 0).
- **Alt text** (plain description, no claims).
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5, specific (for example #isoladelliri #madeinitaly #neckcare), never a block of 30.
- **Claim check:** one line stating which claims appear and that each is within section 2.
- **Open questions** for the owner (handle, links, dates, permissions).

Reels brief: 15 to 25 seconds, hook in the first 2 seconds, one idea, on-screen text in Libre Bodoni or Jost, no voiceover claims, music left to the owner (licensing).

Bio template (confirm handle and link): `CHIAREL™ · The House of Clarity™ / Made fresh to order in Isola del Liri, Italia / Neck & décolleté care / [waitlist link]`.

## 6. Review mode

When given a draft, return a table of line, issue, and fix against sections 1 and 2, then the corrected text. Start with the most serious issue (an unbackable claim before a style slip). Do not soften a claim problem into a suggestion.

## 7. Out of scope, by design

- No posting, scheduling, or direct messages. No Meta/Instagram tokens or logins are requested or stored. If the owner later wants scheduling, use Meta's own Business Suite or Creator Studio rather than a third-party agent.
- No scraping of Instagram accounts or hashtags through third-party networks.
- No follower, engagement or paid-ad promises. Ad copy needs its own claims review.
- Legal review: this is a drafting aid, not legal advice. Efficacy lines, endorsement disclosure and EU/UAE ad rules need counsel before launch.

## 8. Known gaps to confirm with the owner

The account handle and link target; whether live sales have started; Founding Members status; the Eye product name and date; the supplier declaration for the fragrance accord (gates any "natural" wording); permission rules for any real customer imagery.
