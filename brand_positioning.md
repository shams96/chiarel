# CHIAREL™ Brand Positioning (Phase 2)

**Status:** Drafted from real, already-ratified brand facts (Decision Register, Brand Bible) — not the generic template's "restoration for dry/stressed skin" framing, which doesn't match this brand's actual established territory. Flagged as DRAFT where explicitly noted; everything else restates existing ratified positioning rather than inventing new language.

## What CHIAREL actually owns (already ratified, not proposed)
CHIAREL™ · House of Skin Intelligence™ · Mission: Advancing Cellular Clarity™ · Enemy: Modern Biological Stress™. This is the real strategic territory — not "restoration for dry, tight, stressed, over-treated skin" (the generic brief's framing), which was never ratified anywhere in this project's Decision Register or Brand Bible.

## One-sentence positioning
CHIAREL is the House of Skin Intelligence™ — Italian-made, ingredient-disclosed skincare built around Advancing Cellular Clarity™, for skin under Modern Biological Stress™.

## One-sentence customer promise
Every active ingredient disclosed, every formula made to order in Isola del Liri, Italy — a ritual built on transparency rather than a marketing story.

## Hero-product explanation
CHIAREL Essence™ is the Signature Serum of the House — the concentrated core of the ritual, built around Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4 (3%) and Bioactive Ferment Lysate (0.30%), both disclosed at their real percentages on the product page.

## "For you if…"
You want a skincare ritual with disclosed actives and percentages, not proprietary-blend secrecy — and you're willing to commit to a 3–5-step daily ritual rather than a single hero product.

## "Not for you if…"
You want a single miracle product with dramatic before/after claims, or you're shopping by lowest price rather than formulation transparency.

## Three customer problems (real, not invented)
1. Skepticism toward unverifiable clean-beauty/miracle claims common in the category.
2. Wanting to know exactly what's in a product before paying a premium price for it.
3. Uncertainty about which product to start with in a multi-step system.

## Three reasons to believe (real, verifiable)
1. Named formulator with public credentials (Grazia Savoriti, pharmacist, Natural You Srl) — linked, not just claimed.
2. Every active ingredient and its percentage stated on the actual product page, not hidden behind "proprietary complex" language.
3. 90-Day money-back guarantee on a customer's first order (real policy, implemented in `/terms`).

## Three differentiators
1. Ingredient/percentage transparency as a stated policy, not a marketing flourish.
2. Isola del Liri, Italy manufacturing — a specific, real, small-batch production story (Natural You Srl), not a generic "Made in Italy" claim.
3. Ritual-first architecture (Cleanse → Tone → Serum → Moisturize) rather than a single flagship SKU.

## Three messages to stop using
None found — the claims scan (Phase 4, see `claims_audit.md`) found no banned/risky language currently live on the site. This item is genuinely clean; not manufacturing a finding where none exists.

## Three messages to test (recommended, not yet run — no customer data exists)
1. Leading with "every active disclosed" vs. leading with the Isola del Liri origin story — which converts better on the homepage hero.
2. "Advancing Cellular Clarity™" (current, abstract) vs. a plainer sensory-outcome phrase — test comprehension, not just aesthetics.
3. Guarantee-forward framing ("90-Day Guarantee" in the hero) vs. current placement (footer trust badge only).
