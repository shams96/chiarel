## Decided
- Plan hosting fixes inside the current Hostinger Business Web Hosting plan ($203.88/yr, renews 2027-02-17). Active choice is Option D in claudedocs/specs/hosting-move/SPEC-AND-PLAN.md: heartbeat auto-restart, one-merge-at-a-time deploys, evidence capture, decide at renewal.
- Spend on a Cloud plan or VPS is shelved, not rejected; revisit only if the owner says so or outages recur with evidence.

## Tried and ruled out
- Recommending Option A (Hostinger Cloud Startup, ~$120 first year) and asking the owner to buy: the owner said they cannot do that and to consider the current tier only.

## Watch out
- When a scoping or recommendation involves money, ask or check the budget constraint before making a purchase the preferred option; offer a zero-cost path first.
- Never place purchase orders; billing actions are the owner's.
