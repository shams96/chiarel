# CHIAREL mark, round 3 (draft, 2026-10-24)

Brief: CHIAREL comes from the Italian "chiaro" (clear, light). The mark must carry clarity, not just a generic water drop.

## NEW (from the owner reference image): MARK D (APERTURE, 3D)
The owner liked the drop-shaped window with a brushed champagne-gold inner wall in the generated render. Mark D rebuilds that as a mark: an ivory drop-shaped slab with a drop-shaped window; the window shows the brushed gold wall on the left and curving along the base, and clear light through the rest. Clarity = the window; the gold wall = the House.
- 3D hero art: CHIAREL_mark_D_APERTURE_3D_on-ivory.png / .svg, plus _transparent.svg. Built with SVG gradients and a brushed-metal noise filter: a stand-in for a proper 3D render (Blender/Keyshot), not a final.
- Flat versions for small sizes and deboss: CHIAREL_mark_D_APERTURE_flat_*.svg and CHIAREL_mark_D_APERTURE_flat_small-size-test.png (holds at 16 px; the window and the gold crescent still read).

## Previous recommendation: MARK A (LENS)
- **C** = the brand initial, and it extends the debossed C monogram already moulded into the gold caps (RFQ V19, Decision 010).
- **The C is a lens / aperture**: the empty counter is clear glass, the opening lets light in (chiaro).
- **The drop** at its centre = the concentrate held in focus.
- Ring inner radius = 0.618 x outer radius (golden ratio). One colour, no gradients, works as deboss, foil, favicon.
- Files: CHIAREL_mark_A_LENS_*.svg (ink, champagne, on Formal Garden, on ink), icon PNGs 16-512 px, CHIAREL_mark_A_LENS_lockups.png.

## Compared (labelled in the images)
| Label | Idea | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A LENS | C ring with a centred drop | holds at 16 px, deboss reads, best fit with the cap monogram |
| B IN THE GAP | drop shifted toward the opening | no gain over A, balance worse |
| C POINTING OUT | drop rotated to point out of the opening | reads as a pointer or an "e"; rejected |
| 1 CLEAR TO CONCENTRATE | outlined (clear) top, solid bottom | strongest meaning, but reads as the letter A / a flask; rejected |
| 2 SEE THROUGH | line displaced inside a drop outline (refraction) | conceptually best, too fine for 16 px; rejected |
| 4 RAY CUT | earlier tall drop with a diagonal cut | good silhouette, no "C", no clarity idea |

## Not done / risks
- Needs a trademark and similarity search: C-plus-drop marks are common in skincare. Not searched.
- The ring and drop are drawn from geometry, not optically tuned by a type designer; a professional pass (stroke contrast, terminal angle) is still worth paying for.
- Not placed on the website or packaging. Earlier drop-only rounds are in archive-round1-2-drop-only/.
