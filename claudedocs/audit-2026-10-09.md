## Dependencies

`npm audit` today: 20 vulnerabilities in total (3 critical, 12 high, 5 moderate). Production dependencies only: 10 (1 critical, 7 high, 2 moderate). The critical production issue is Next.js 14; the high ones are in its tooling chain (`postcss`, `micromatch`, `braces`, `fast-glob`, `chokidar`, `source-map-js`, `tailwindcss`). Hostinger's scanner reported a larger number (46) because it counts differently. The real fix is a planned upgrade of Next.js (14 to 15 or 16), `eslint-config-next`, Vitest (2 to a current major) and Tailwind, followed by a full regression pass. `npm audit fix --force` should not be run blindly: it makes those major jumps without testing.

Also behind a major version: React 18 (19 available), Prisma 6.12 (7 available), `framer-motion`, `@types/*`. None is urgent.

## Security spot-checks (not a full pentest)

- Admin routes: every handler uses `withRole`. Checkout and signup are rate-limited. Webhook signature is verified. Headers and CSP are set in `middleware.ts`.
- Customer-facing results photos are anonymized and carry a testing disclosure.
- Not covered here: a live attack pass on production, dependency exploitability, backups and restore. Run `predeploy-security-audit` before switching Stripe to live.
